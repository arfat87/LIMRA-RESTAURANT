Our Story

About LIMRA

Also known as Menara Bibi, a beloved culinary destination in the heart of Egra

Nestled in the vibrant town of Egra, LIMRA Restaurant — fondly known as Menara Bibi — is more than just a place to eat. It's an experience crafted with love, tradition, and the finest ingredients from the heart of West Bengal.

From sizzling tandoori platters to aromatic biryanis, our kitchen brings together the rich heritage of Indian cuisine with modern presentation. Every dish tells a story, every bite carries warmth — because here, food is family.

With 4.8 stars on Google and the trust of our community, we're proud to be Egra's go-to destination for celebrations, family meals, and everyday moments worth savoring.
