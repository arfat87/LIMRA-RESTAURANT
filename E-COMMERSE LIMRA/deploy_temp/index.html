Our Story

About LIMRA

Also known as SK Arif — a cherished culinary destination in the heart of Egra

Nestled in the vibrant town of Egra, LIMRA Restaurant — fondly known as SK Arif — is more than just a place to eat. It's an experience crafted with love, tradition, and the finest ingredients.

From sizzling tandoori platters to aromatic biryanis, our kitchen brings together the rich heritage of Indian cuisine with modern presentation. Every dish tells a story — because here, food is family.

With 4.8★ on Google and the trust of our community, we're proud to be Egra's go-to destination for celebrations, family meals, and everyday moments.
