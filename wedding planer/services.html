Exquisite Decoration & Floristry

We transform raw banquet halls into dreamlands of lights, drapes, and florals. Collaborating with high-end designers and florists, we construct majestic mandaps, pathway floral carpets, pastel drapes, glassmorphic stage columns, and glowing chandeliers. We balance traditional accents (like marigolds and brass lamps) with modern setups to create visual masterpieces.

  • Thematic Stage & Canopy (Mandap) Decors
  • Imported Floral Arrangements & Table Centerpieces
  • LED Ambient Uplighting & Traditional Hanging Fixtures
  • Exquisite Entryway Gates & Photo Booth Backdrops

Sangeet & Entertainment Coordinating

The Sangeet ceremony is the soul of Indian wedding celebrations. We direct the entire event: building grand LED backdrops, aligning sound grids, contracting charismatic anchors, hiring professional DJs, coordinating choreography blocks for family dances, and managing live musical artists.

  • LED Dance Stage Designs & Sound Configurations
  • Professional Emcees, Choreographers & DJs
  • Vibrant Backdrop Visuals & Special Effects (Fog, Pyros)
  • Artist Contracting & Concert-level Coordination
