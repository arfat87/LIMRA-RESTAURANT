Our Journey

We Paint Memories On The Canvas of Time

Founded in 2011, Khokan Studio started with a single, passionate objective: to design wedding events that encapsulate the authentic emotions, cultural heritage, and modern aspirations of every couple. From a small decoration consulting desk, we grew into an award-winning boutique wedding organizer managing over 250+ luxurious celebrations nationwide.

We believe that a wedding is not simply a checklist of items to coordinate; it is a sacred, once-in-a-lifetime narration of a couple's journey. By blending contemporary aesthetics, glassmorphic styling, rich florals, and flawless logistics, we transform coordinates into fairytales.

Our core crew features seasoned banquet coordinators, graphic designers, logistics specialists, and five-star culinary experts, all coordinated harmoniously under our founder's vision.

What Guides Us

Our Core Wedding Philosophies

Bespoke Styling

We never duplicate designs. Every floral alignment, table linen choice, and entryway arch is custom illustrated to match your unique persona.

Eco-Luxury Focus

We prioritize locally sourced organic blooms, biodegradable materials, and energy-efficient lighting grids without compromising grand aesthetics.

Absolute Transparency

No hidden bills or shock commissions. We work under flat consultation rates or open budgeting sheets, keeping you in charge of costs.
